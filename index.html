Install Prod Calc

Add Prod Calc to your phone’s Home Screen for quick access.

iPhone / iPad · Safari
  1. Open Prod Calc in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm Prod Calc and tap Add.
Android · Chrome
  1. Open Prod Calc in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the installation.
